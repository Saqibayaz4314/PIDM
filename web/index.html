Bring downloads into your browser

Add the PIDM extension to Chrome or Edge. Then send files and choose video qualities without leaving your browser.

  1. 1
    Load the browser extension

    Open the extension folder. In your browser's Extensions page, enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked.

  2. 2
    Open PIDM

    Keep this desktop app open. The extension connects automatically; no setup code is needed.

  3. 3
    You're ready to download

    Refresh your video page, then use the PIDM toolbar icon or the button over the player.
