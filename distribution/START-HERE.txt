PIDM - Windows 10/11, 64-bit

1. Run PIDM-Setup-0.3.0-x64.exe and finish the installer.
2. Open PIDM from the desktop or Start menu.
3. Paste a direct file URL, or paste a video URL and click Video qualities.
4. Select an available quality and add the download.

Node.js, Python, yt-dlp and FFmpeg do not need separate installation.
Internet is needed to download files/videos, not to install the bundled tools.

Optional Chrome/Edge integration (one time on each computer):
- In PIDM click Open extension folder.
- Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions in your browser.
- Enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked and select the opened folder.
- Keep PIDM open. The extension connects automatically when downloading.
- Updating from 0.5.0: remove the old extension and Load unpacked once again.
- On a video page click the extension toolbar icon to choose a quality.

Downloads are saved in your Windows Downloads\Personal Downloader folder.
Remove cancels an unfinished job and removes its entry; completed files stay.
Uninstalling the app does not remove downloaded files.

The installer is not digitally signed. It has not been verified on every PC.
Website support varies; 403/429 errors, login requirements and unsupported
streams can still prevent downloads. DRM-protected content is unsupported.
